Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

M.I. ENGINEERING WORKS respects the privacy of our customers, website visitors, and registered users. This Privacy Policy explains the types of personal information we may collect through our website, why we use it, and how we handle and protect that information.

By submitting personal information or uploading technical files through this website, you acknowledge that your data will be handled as described in this Privacy Policy.

1 Information We Collect

Account Credentials

Name, email address, phone number, company name, account roles, and email verification status. Passwords are always stored using secure hashing mechanisms.

Enquiry & Order Details

Contact information, products of interest, quantities, technical specifications, enquiry remarks, and business communication history.

Technical Files & Drawings

Uploaded blueprints, technical drawings, CAD files, PDFs, spreadsheets, and reference engineering materials supplied by your organization.

Technical & Server Logs

IP addresses, browser type, device information, access timestamps, requested pages, security logs, and functional session cookies.

2 How We Use Information & Email Updates

We process your information exclusively for legitimate business purposes, including processing engineering enquiries, generating quotations, managing customer accounts, and maintaining system security.

Transactional Email Notifications

We send transactional emails necessary for operational updates. These include:

  • Account verifications & password resets
  • Enquiry confirmation & status changes
  • Quotations & official business documents
  • Security alerts & service notices

3 Security Measures & Technical Drawings

We employ HTTPS encryption, strict database access controls, password hashing, and secure server monitoring to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

Customer Document Policy: Technical drawings and uploaded specs remain your property. Uploading files grants M.I. ENGINEERING WORKS permission to evaluate, store, and process them strictly for manufacturing evaluation, quotation, and fulfillment.

4 Information Sharing, Retention & Cookies

  • We Never Sell Data: Information is never sold to third parties. We share data only with essential infrastructure providers (hosting, email platforms, cloud storage) bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Data Retention: Information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill services, resolve disputes, satisfy accounting or tax laws, and maintain business records.
  • Essential Cookies: We use strictly necessary cookies to maintain secure authenticated user sessions and preserve website preferences.

5 Your Rights & Account Choices

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or updating of your personal information stored in your account.

Account Deletion: You may submit an account deletion request at any time. Note that certain transactional records, tax invoices, and legal compliance history may be retained even after account deletion as permitted or required by law.

External Links & Children's Privacy: Our site is intended for adult business representatives. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party external websites linked on our platform.

Privacy Enquiries & Data Requests

Contact our privacy coordinator for questions or data update requests.
