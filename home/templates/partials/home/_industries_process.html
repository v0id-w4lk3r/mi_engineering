Industries We Serve

Industrial Fasteners for Every Critical Sector

Our precision-engineered bolts, studs, and custom components are trusted across infrastructure, heavy manufacturing, energy, and automotive sectors across India.

General Manufacturing & OEM

Hex bolts, socket head screws, and precision nuts for production machinery, assembly lines, and OEM component supply chains requiring consistent quality across large batches.

Construction & Civil Infrastructure

L-type and J-type anchor bolts, high-strength structural tie rods, and foundation fasteners for bridges, flyovers, commercial structures, and pre-engineered buildings.

Heavy Machinery & Equipment

Precision-turned custom components, socket head fasteners, and high-tensile studs for heavy machinery builders requiring exact-tolerance machined parts from technical drawings.

Automotive Engineering

High-fatigue-resistant threaded fasteners, precision-turned pins, and custom bush components for automotive sub-assembly and vehicle manufacturing applications.

Power, Energy & Oil-Gas

ASTM A193 B7 stud bolts, A194 2H heavy hex nuts, and SS316 corrosion-resistant fasteners for power distribution, oil refineries, and renewable energy structures.

Heavy Fabrication & Steel Structures

Large-diameter stud bolts (M24–M64), custom coupling nuts, and heavy plain washers for fabricated steel structures, pressure vessels, and industrial storage tanks.
