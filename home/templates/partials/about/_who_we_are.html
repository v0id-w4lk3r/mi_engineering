Who We Are

Engineering Components Built Around Your Exact Specifications

M.I. Engineering Works was established to fill the gap between off-the-shelf fasteners and genuinely specification-driven industrial component supply. We work directly with procurement engineers, project managers, and OEM buyers to supply both catalog-standard and fully custom-machined components.

Selecting the right engineering component requires accurate material grading, precise dimensional tolerances, and strict compliance with operational standards. Our technical team reviews your drawings and specifications directly — eliminating intermediaries and ensuring every component meets your exact requirement.

From M3 bolts for precision assemblies to M64 anchor bolts for civil infrastructure — from Grade 8.8 structural fasteners to ASTM A193 B7 high-pressure stud bolts — we manufacture and supply components with full material traceability and EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificates.

Precision Manufacturing

Micron-level tolerances maintained across all thread profiles, dimensional specifications, and surface finishes — from prototype to bulk production.

Material Quality Assurance

Chemical composition verification, dimensional inspection, and hardness testing across every production batch — with EN 10204 3.1 MTC available.

Drawing-Based Custom Components

CNC-turned and machined custom parts produced directly from your CAD files, PDF blueprints, or technical specifications — no minimum order restriction on custom parts.

Reliable Supply & On-Time Delivery

Committed lead times, transparent production tracking, and dedicated account management for repeat and project-based procurement.
